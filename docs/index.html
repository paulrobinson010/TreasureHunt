Treasure Hunt

Get the kids out and about!

How it works

🗺️

Make a hunt

Drop treasure points on a map, name the hunt, and write down the prize. It stays secret until the hunt is solved.

💬

Send it

Share the hunt over iMessage, WhatsApp or anything else. No accounts, no servers — the whole hunt travels inside the link.

🧭

Hunt it down

The map shows rough zones, never exact spots. Get within 25 m and a compass appears — point the phone the right way and it buzzes and pings. Find every point and the prize is revealed!

Get the app

Treasure Hunt is currently in family testing and installed directly by the hunt maker. If someone sent you a hunt link, ask them to set the app up on your phone — then tap the link again and the hunt jumps straight in.

No accounts. No tracking. No servers. Hunts are encrypted and live only on your phones and inside the links you share.
