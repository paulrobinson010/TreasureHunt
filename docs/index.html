X-Marks

Get the kids out and about!

How it works

1

🗺️ Make a hunt

Drop treasure points on a map, name the hunt, and write down the prize. It stays secret until the hunt is solved.

2

💬 Send it

Share over iMessage, WhatsApp or anything else. No accounts, no servers — the whole hunt travels inside an encrypted link.

3

🧭 Hunt it down

The map shows rough zones, never exact spots. Get close and a compass appears to point the way.

Point the phone the right way and it BUZZES 📳 … walk the treasure down and it PINGS ✨ … find every X and the PRIZE IS REVEALED! 🏆

Get X-Marks

X-Marks is currently in family testing and installed directly by the hunt maker. If someone sent you a hunt link, ask them to set the app up on your phone — then tap the link again and the hunt jumps straight in.

No accounts. No tracking. No servers. Hunts are encrypted and live only on your phones and inside the links you share.
